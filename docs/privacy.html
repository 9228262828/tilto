TILTO PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

Summary: TILTO is an offline-first balance game. The current core version does not require an account, Firebase, a backend, cloud sync, ads, analytics, online leaderboards, multiplayer, location permission, or motion-sensor permission.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how TILTO, package com.tilto.balancegame, handles information in the current core version of the app.

2. Scope

This Policy applies to the TILTO mobile application and these informational pages.

3. Offline-First Design

TILTO is designed as an offline-first casual balance game. The current core gameplay does not require an internet connection.

4. No Account Required

You do not need to create a TILTO account, username, password, or personal profile to play the current core version.

5. No Login Data

Because there is no login system in the current core version, TILTO does not request sign-in credentials.

6. Gameplay Progress

The app may store gameplay progress locally so that records and statistics remain available between sessions.

7. Endless Best Score

TILTO may store your highest score for Endless mode locally on your device.

8. 60 Sec Best Score

TILTO may store your highest score for 60 Sec Challenge mode locally on your device.

9. One Fall Best Score

TILTO may store your highest score for One Fall mode locally on your device.

10. Games Played

The app may store the total number of completed gameplay runs.

11. Landed Pieces

The app may store the total number of pieces that landed successfully during gameplay.

12. Perfect Balances

The app may store the number of Perfect Balance bonuses you achieved.

13. Current Score

Scores displayed during a run are calculated locally on the device.

14. Lives

In Endless mode, remaining lives are gameplay state and are calculated locally.

15. Game Timer

The 60-second challenge timer is calculated locally and is not used to track you outside the game.

16. Platform Tilt

Left and right tilt input is used only to control gameplay.

17. Simplified Game Physics

TILTO uses simplified in-app motion and balance logic rather than collecting motion-sensor data from your device.

18. No Motion Sensor Requirement

The current core version does not require accelerometer, gyroscope, or motion-sensor permissions.

19. Piece Generation

Falling pieces and their motion values are generated locally on your device.

20. Difficulty Progression

Spawn speed and gameplay pressure may increase as a run continues.

21. Dark Mode Preference

Your dark-mode choice may be stored locally so TILTO can remember your appearance preference.

22. Haptic Preference

Your haptic-feedback setting may be stored locally so TILTO can remember whether vibration feedback is enabled.

23. Haptic Feedback

When enabled, TILTO may use standard device haptic feedback for gameplay interactions. This does not require access to your personal information.

24. Local Storage

The current core version uses local application storage, such as SharedPreferences, for preferences, scores, best results, and statistics.

25. No Firebase

The current core version is not designed to use Firebase for authentication, databases, analytics, remote configuration, or cloud synchronization.

26. No Remote Backend

The current core version does not send your gameplay records to a TILTO-operated backend.

27. No Cloud Sync

The current core version does not provide TILTO-operated cloud synchronization between devices.

28. No Advertising

The current core version does not contain TILTO-operated advertising.

29. No Personalized Ads

TILTO does not use your gameplay records to deliver personalized advertising in the current core version.

30. No Behavioral Analytics

The current core version is not designed to collect behavioral analytics for profiling, marketing, or advertising.

31. No Cross-App Tracking

TILTO is not designed to track your activity across unrelated third-party apps or websites.

32. No Sale of Personal Information

TILTO does not intentionally sell or rent the locally stored gameplay information described in this Policy.

33. No Precise Location

The current core version does not require precise or approximate device location permission.

34. No Camera

The current core version does not require camera access.

35. No Microphone

The current core version does not require microphone access or audio recording.

36. No Contacts

The current core version does not require access to your contacts.

37. No Phone or SMS

The current core version does not require phone-call, call-log, or SMS permissions.

38. No Calendar

The current core version does not require access to your device calendar.

39. No Photos or Media

The current core version does not require access to your photo library, gallery, videos, or media library.

40. No General File Access

The current core version does not require access to your personal documents or general file storage for normal gameplay.

41. No Payment Information

The current core version does not require payment-card information, bank credentials, or payment account details.

42. No Purchases in Core Version

The current core version described by this Policy does not require an in-app purchase to play its core modes.

43. No Social Features

The current core version does not include public profiles, social feeds, direct messaging, friend lists, or player-to-player chat.

44. No Multiplayer

The current core version does not require multiplayer networking.

45. No Online Leaderboards

The current core version does not upload scores to an online leaderboard.

46. No User-Generated Public Content

The current core version does not allow users to post public text, images, video, or other content to other players.

47. No Sensitive Data Requirement

TILTO does not require health information, government identifiers, payment credentials, passwords, or other sensitive personal information to play.

48. Data Retention

Saved gameplay records generally remain on your device until you reset progress, clear application storage, uninstall the app, or the operating system removes the data.

49. Reset Progress

TILTO includes a reset option that can remove saved scores and gameplay statistics from the app's local storage.

50. Uninstalling the App

Uninstalling TILTO may remove locally stored information, subject to operating-system or device backup and restore behavior.

51. Device Backups

Your operating system or device provider may back up application data according to your device settings. Such backups are not controlled by TILTO's core app logic.

52. Data Recovery

Because the current core version does not include TILTO-operated cloud backup, we may not be able to recover deleted progress.

53. Device Security

You are responsible for protecting access to your device using available security features such as a passcode, PIN, password, or biometric lock.

54. Children

TILTO is a general casual game and is not designed to collect personal information from children through accounts, messaging, public profiles, or online social features.

55. Third-Party Platforms

App stores and device platforms may independently process account, device, installation, security, purchase, or diagnostic information under their own privacy policies.

56. Google Play

If you obtain TILTO through Google Play, Google may process installation, account, device, security, purchase, or diagnostic information under Google's own policies and terms.

57. GitHub Pages

These informational pages may be hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary web-server information according to its own policies. These pages do not require you to submit TILTO gameplay data.

58. Support Communications

If you contact us at umarwaseem270@gmail.com, your email provider and our email provider will process the information you voluntarily include in your message.

59. Support Safety

Do not send passwords, payment credentials, private keys, government identifiers, or other highly sensitive information in support emails.

60. Legal Requirements

Information voluntarily provided through support communications may be disclosed if required to comply with applicable law or valid legal process.

61. International Use

You may use TILTO in different countries. Local gameplay data remains on your device in the current core version, while app stores, email providers, and website hosts may process their own data in other jurisdictions.

62. Security

We aim to minimize data handling by keeping current core gameplay local. No method of device storage or electronic processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

63. Changes to the App

The app may receive updates that improve gameplay, design, compatibility, accessibility, or stability.

64. Future Online Features

If future versions add accounts, cloud sync, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, purchases, online leaderboards, multiplayer, social features, or additional permissions, this Policy should be reviewed and updated before release.

65. Future Permissions

If a future feature requires a new device permission, TILTO should request it only when needed for that feature and the Privacy Policy should be updated accordingly.

66. Your Choices

You may change app preferences, disable haptic feedback, reset local progress, clear app storage, uninstall TILTO, or stop using the app.

67. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy when TILTO changes or when legal, technical, distribution, or app-store requirements change.

68. Effective Date

This Privacy Policy is effective as of September 25, 2026.

69. Contact

For privacy questions about TILTO, contact umarwaseem270@gmail.com.
